Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (February 19, 1627 – April 3, 1680) was not just any ruler; he was a Divine incarnation sent to safeguard Hindu Dharma. He founded Hindavi Swarajya — a sovereign Hindu state — with Kshatradharma, guidance from Saints like Samarth Ramdas Swami, and the ideals taught by his mother Jijabai. Against Mughal oppression, he constructed impregnable forts, built a navy to shield our shores, employed ganimi kava (guerrilla warfare) to defeat numerically superior forces, decisively ended the terror of Afzal Khan in a display of wits and valour, and established an ideal Kingdom by protecting temples, scriptures, Brahmins, women, and Gau Mata. He outlawed cow slaughter in his kingdom and adopted the title Go-Brahmana-Pratipalaka, that is, the protector of cows and Brahmins.

  • Upbringing: His mother, Jijabai, deeply shaped his character with values of courage, justice, and devotion to Swarajya (self-rule)
